Wherever you find a Foodie's Corner, our goal stays the same.

To make your visit easy and enjoyable, whether you're grabbing a quick meal, filling up your tank, picking up essentials, or simply stopping in to say hello.

Where it started

It all started at Whitestone.

Over 25 years ago, our journey began not as owners, but as employees learning every aspect of the business from the ground up.

We mopped floors, stocked shelves, and learned that true service means remembering a regular's name and anticipating their needs. Those early days at the Whitestone location laid the foundation for everything Foodie's Corner is today.
